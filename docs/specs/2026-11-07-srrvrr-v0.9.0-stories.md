# SRRVRR v0.9.0, the transfer monitor: proposed stories

Status: proposed with the design (`docs/specs/2026-11-07-srrvrr-v0.9.0-design.md`), 2026-11-07, for the owner to
approve. Each becomes one issue in milestone v0.9.0 and one PR. Test ids use the prefixes below until the issues
have numbers. Ordered so the automated-test-heavy parts come first and the parts needing the owner's elevated UAT
come last.

## 1. The bottleneck and the shares (T21a-)
- Read the two SMB counter categories in the fast loop (first read before the loop), plus `% Idle Time` and
  `Current Disk Queue Length` from the drive counters and `Current Bandwidth` from the network counters it already
  reads. `Win32_Share` every 60 s for share-to-drive.
- The bottleneck rule (design section 2) as a pure function; share rows; the `smbRead` and `smbWrite` history
  lines and graph; `transfers.available`, `bottleneck` and `shares` in `/stats`; `BottleneckPercent`.
- Tests: recorded counter fixtures; every bottleneck case; the tick budget measured.
- UAT, unelevated on the server: a large loopback copy into a scratch folder on the code drive's share.

## 2. Write bursts (T21b-)
- The per-PC burst detector on the per-session counters (design section 4); `write-burst` and
  `write-burst-ended`, keyed per burst; the ended event on a clean stop; `BurstFiles`, `BurstWindowSeconds`,
  `BurstQuietMinutes`. Until story 3, a burst's PC is "a PC (needs administrator)".
- Tests: start, window, quiet-time merging, the pair always complete, Maintenance marking, the daily cap.
- UAT, unelevated on the server: a loopback copy of 600 small files; both events in the Flagged list.

## 3. PCs and folders (T21c-)
- The session and open-file CIM readers in the slow sampler (5 s busy, 30 s idle), the session-to-PC join,
  folder trimming (no file names, ever), `NamedPCs` and `TransferFolders` with validation, the PC rows,
  `unknown-pc` and `unknown-pc-write`, the "needs administrator" state.
- Tests: hand-made CIM fixtures; a test that no fixture file name reaches `/stats` or a log; every setting rule.
- A small fixture recorder (names, addresses, accounts and paths replaced) for the owner to run once, elevated.
- UAT: elevated, by the owner, together with story 4.

## 4. Maintenance limited to named PCs (T21d-)
- The address match (IPv4-mapped, zone ids, loopback and the server's own addresses), the 403 and its counted
  refusals, `viewer` in `/stats`, the disabled checkbox and its message, the "only at the server" state when no PC
  is named; M8 in the test plan narrowed.
- Tests: the match table, the check order, the page states.
- UAT (about 15 minutes, elevated test build, the owner): from a named PC and an unnamed one, stories 3 and 4.
